- F16 (in progress): **automated team email** - the team gets told about its own programme, with a per-member opt-out per kind. Timing lives in pure rules (`server/utils/notification-rules.ts`): **match-info 48 h** before a match, **training reminder 24 h** before, **absence nudge 4 h** before, each due from the lead moment until kick-off and **never at or after it** (a dispatcher that was down does not send stale mail), and never for a cancelled session; a training gets the reminder and a match the match-info, so one session never produces both. Recipients are the team's players, the **active** parents of those players (mailed about that child) and the **active** staff, each filtered on their own opt-out column and de-duplicated so a player-coach is mailed once. Time-based sending runs from `dispatchDueNotifications` via a Nitro interval plugin (15 min, first pass 30 s after boot, non-overlapping, off under `MAIL_DISPATCH_DISABLED` and in tests) and is **idempotent through a `sent_notifications` ledger** written *before* the send - a restart or a double tick can never mail a team twice, at the deliberate cost of possibly losing a single mail. Absence nudges skip players who already reported, and their parents with them. Change, cancellation and reinstatement notices are **event-driven** instead: fired from `updateSession` and from a club closure's bulk cancel, they list only the fields that actually moved (silent when an edit changes nothing a member would notice), carry the cancellation reason, and are wrapped so a mail failure can never roll back the schedule change that triggered it. Account page grew an **E-mailvoorkeuren** card; `PATCH /api/me/mail-settings`; migration 0012 (four opt-out columns on `user`, defaulting to opted **in** since these are operational mails, plus the ledger table). Test-set covers the window boundaries (exactly-at-lead, at kick-off, after), the training/match split, Dutch copy incl. home-side-first match titles and the null-location wording, recipient resolution (pending staff excluded, parent-child subject, per-kind opt-out), dispatcher idempotency, the nudge/absence interaction, and all four event notices incl. the bulk-closure fan-out (25 tests).

- F31 (in progress): **secure first-run** - closes the F18 security review's CRITICAL (unauthenticated instance-admin takeover window on a fresh public deploy).
